# Legal Guidelines for Contour

Contour is designed exclusively for lawful reverse engineering.

## Permitted Uses
- Analyzing software you lawfully possess or are explicitly authorized to study
- Interoperability research under applicable exceptions (e.g., DMCA §1201(f) in the US, Article 6 of the EU Software Directive)
- Clean-room documentation of observed external behavior
- Security research with proper authorization and disclosure practices
- Reconstruction of public or abandoned protocols

## Prohibited Uses
- Circumventing technical protection measures (TPMs) beyond what is strictly necessary and lawful
- Copying protected expression or producing substantially similar source code
- Violating EULAs or contracts that validly restrict reverse engineering
- Analyzing systems you do not own or lack authorization for
- Any activity that constitutes unauthorized access under computer fraud laws

## Clean-Room Practice
When contributing models or implementations:
1. Document the signals observed
2. Keep the analysis team separate from any implementation team if recreating functionality
3. Produce original expression based on functional understanding, not copied code

## Disclaimer
This project does not provide legal advice. Users are solely responsible for ensuring their use complies with applicable laws, licenses, and contracts in their jurisdiction. Aura 51 and the maintainers assume no liability for misuse.
